The Problem

Trading with a stranger has always needed a middleman.

A freelancer and a client. A buyer and a seller who've never met. Someone has to hold the money until the work is actually done — today, that someone is a bank, a platform, or blind trust.

  • —Banks and payment platforms exclude the exact people who need a trusted third party most — the ones without accounts, credit history, or a platform's approval.
  • —Blind trust between strangers gets people burned, every day, in every informal market.
  • —Crypto was built to solve exactly this — and then made itself unusable for anyone without a computer science background. Seed phrases, gas fees, and wallet jargon are a wall, not a bridge.
The Solution

The deal holds itself honest.

Two people agree on a deal. The buyer's funds go into a smart contract — not a company's bank account, not an app's database, not our servers. Neither side has to trust the other, or us.

If the seller delivers, the buyer confirms and the contract releases the funds automatically. If the buyer goes quiet, the contract releases the funds to the seller anyway after seven days — no one gets to hold someone else's money hostage.

How It Works

One contract. Four moves.

  1. Create

    A seller lists a deal — open to any buyer, or locked to one specific wallet for a private arrangement.

  2. Fund

    The buyer deposits. Funds move into the contract itself — never into anyone's pocket.

  3. Ship

    The seller marks the work delivered or the goods shipped.

  4. Release

    The buyer confirms receipt. Funds move to the seller instantly, on-chain, minus a 1% platform fee.

If the buyer never confirms, anyone can trigger the same release after a 7-day window — funds never get stuck on a party who's gone silent.
Endless Utility

The Roadmap

The MVP proves the trust model. The roadmap makes it invisible.

Everything from here is about removing every reason a non-technical person would say no.

Shield non-technical users from crypto entirely
  • Sponsored gas via paymasters — a first-time user shouldn't need to already own crypto to complete their first deal
  • Social login (Google, Apple, phone number) backed by an embedded wallet — no seed phrase, no private key, no "connect MetaMask"
  • Full fiat on/off-ramp — deposit and withdraw in local currency; the platform handles conversion behind the scenes
  • Stablecoin settlement — no one should need to think in ETH to sell a chicken or a website
Close the remaining trust gaps
  • Server-side verification of on-chain state, replacing today's client-reported transaction records
  • A dispute-resolution interface for the arbitration the contract already supports
  • An independent security audit before any mainnet deployment holds real funds
Scale
  • Mainnet deployment once GIWA's mainnet is live
  • On-chain reputation, so trust compounds across deals instead of resetting every time
  • The next utility dApp in the Ubuntu Pay family
